Humble Cows - Custom ERP Solution

A comprehensive ERP system designed to streamline dairy operations, from milk procurement to inventory management and financial reporting.

📊

Project Overview

Humble Cows is an initiative in the Food, FMCG, and Agriculture sector, established in 2018. The firm provides dairy products including dairy feeds, ghee, and milk to bulk dairy corporations. To address the complex procurement and management needs of the dairy industry, Humble Cows partnered with Hackerkernel to develop a custom ERP system.

Our goal was to create a comprehensive web portal and mobile app that could link various processes: from milk fat testing machine reports to farmer databases, and monthly profit reports. The system had to be simple and intuitive for farmers while maintaining enterprise-level functionality.
